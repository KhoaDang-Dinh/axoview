# Organising a diagram

## Layers

<!-- keywords: hide, show, visibility, group items, layer -->

Open the **Layers** panel from the left dock. **Add layer** (+) creates a layer;
items are assigned with **Assign to layer** in the context menu, or by dragging
them in the panel. Items with no layer appear under **Unassigned**; drop items
there to unassign them.

Hide a layer to hide everything on it. Deleting a layer asks what to do with its
contents: **Keep contents** (they become unassigned and visible) or **Delete
contents too**.

## Stacking order

<!-- keywords: front, back, z-order, on top, behind, overlap order -->

When items overlap, the context menu's **Bring forward**, **Send backward**,
**Bring to front** and **Send to back** change which is on top. Shortcuts:
**Ctrl+]** brings forward and **Ctrl+[** sends backward; add **Shift** to jump
to the front or back.

## Copy, cut, paste and duplicate

<!-- keywords: copy, paste, clone, duplicate -->

**Ctrl+C** copies and **Ctrl+X** cuts the selection; **Ctrl+V** pastes at the
mouse position, offset so it does not land on top of the original. Connectors
between copied items are copied with them. **Duplicate** in the context menu
copies and pastes in one step.

## Snap to grid and collision

<!-- keywords: overlap, collision, grid, snap, free placement -->

By default items snap to the grid and do not overlap. In the context menu,
**Unsnap from grid** lets an item sit between grid points, and **Disable
collision** lets it overlap others. **Enable snap to grid** / **Disable snap to
grid** in the canvas menu change the default for new items.
