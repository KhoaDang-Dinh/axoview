# Sharing, presenting and exporting

## Sharing a view-only link

<!-- keywords: share, read only, link, team, colleague, publish -->

Save the diagram, then click **Share**. Axoview creates a link; **Copy** puts it
on the clipboard. Anyone with the link can view the diagram read-only: they see
the saved snapshot and cannot edit it or see your other diagrams. **Copy share
link** in a diagram's menu in the Diagrams panel copies the link again later.

## Presenting

<!-- keywords: present, meeting, slideshow, demo, fullscreen -->

**Present** (or **Save & Present** when there are unsaved changes) shows the
diagram without the editing tools. In present mode you can switch **Layers** on
and off (**Solo** shows one layer alone), **Hide labels**, and click an item to
see its details. **Back to editing** returns to the editor.

## Annotating

<!-- keywords: draw, pen, highlight, mark up, annotate -->

The **Annotate** pen (top right of the canvas) opens a palette with **Pencil**,
**Highlighter**, **Line**, **Arrow**, **Rectangle**, **Ellipse**, **Eraser**,
**Undo**, **Redo** and **Clear all**. Annotations are for pointing things out
while presenting: they follow pan and zoom, stay until you clear them or reload
the page, and are never saved into the diagram or its exports.

## Exporting an image

<!-- keywords: png, svg, picture, screenshot, image -->

**Export** → **Export Image** opens the image export. Choose the **Angle** (as
viewed, or the page default), **Show grid**, **Show labels**, **Background
color** or **Transparent background**, **Crop to content** and the **Export
Quality (DPI)**, then **Download as PNG** or **Download as SVG**. Chrome or Edge
give the best results.

## Exporting a diagram file or a whole project

<!-- keywords: backup, zip, all diagrams, download, json, save a copy -->

- **Export** → **Export JSON** downloads the open diagram as a `.json` file.
- **Export** → **Export Project (.zip)**, or **Export project** in the Diagrams
  panel, downloads every diagram in a folder structure as one `.zip`.

Use these to keep a copy outside Axoview or to move diagrams to another site.

## Importing

<!-- keywords: import, open file, upload diagram, json, zip -->

**Import** in the Diagrams panel (or on the start screen) loads a diagram `.json`
or a project `.zip` exported from Axoview. Anything else is rejected with
"This file isn't a valid Axoview diagram."
