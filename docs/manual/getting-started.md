# Getting started

## What Axoview is

Axoview is a diagram editor for isometric (3D-looking) and flat 2D diagrams of
cloud, network and software architecture. It ships with general icons and AWS,
Google Cloud, Azure and Kubernetes icon packs. You draw by placing items (icons),
joining them with connectors, and grouping them with rectangles, text boxes and
labels.

## Signing in

<!-- keywords: login, log in, sign out, account, password, google -->

On the hosted Axoview site the editor opens at `/app` and asks you to sign in
first. Sign in with your Axoview account, or with Google when the sign-in page
offers it. Your diagrams are then kept on the server under your account, so they
are there the next time you sign in from any browser.

To sign out, open the account menu (your user name, top right) and choose
**Sign out**.

## The Diagrams panel

The left dock holds four panels: **File explorer** (Diagrams), **Elements**,
**Layers** and **Settings**. The Diagrams panel lists your diagrams under
**This server**. Its toolbar has **New diagram**, **New folder**, **Import**,
**Export project**, **Refresh** and **Collapse all**.

- Click a diagram to open it.
- Right-click a diagram for **Open**, **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Copy share
  link**, **Export as image…**, **Export as JSON** and **Delete**.
- Right-click a folder for **New diagram**, **New folder**, **Rename**,
  **Export folder…** and **Delete**. Deleting a folder also deletes the
  diagrams inside it; Axoview asks first.

The Elements and Layers panels say "open or create a diagram first" until a
diagram is open.

## Creating a diagram

<!-- keywords: new diagram, start, create, blank -->

Click **New diagram** in the Diagrams panel (or on the empty start screen). Give
it a name; you can rename it later from the page tabs (pencil, **Rename
diagram**) or with **F2** when nothing is selected.

## Saving

<!-- keywords: save, autosave, unsaved, keep my work -->

The status area at the top shows whether the open diagram is saved: **Saved at**
a time, **Modified** or **Unsaved**. Press **Ctrl+S** (Cmd+S on Mac) or use
**Save** to save it. **Ctrl+O** (Cmd+O) opens a diagram.

## Pages

<!-- keywords: page, tab, another page, multiple pages -->

A diagram can have up to five pages, shown as tabs along the bottom of the
canvas. Each page is a separate drawing that shares the diagram's items list.

- **Add page** (the + after the tabs) adds one; at five pages the button says
  "Page limit reached (5)".
- The pencil on a tab renames it; the bin, **Delete page**, removes it.
